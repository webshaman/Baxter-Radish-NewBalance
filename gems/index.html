Shortlisted Artist — 01 / 03

John Nathaniel

John Nathaniel is a Grammy-nominated producer, songwriter, and mixer known for blending live strings, orchestral arrangements, acoustic instrumentation, and electronic production into cinematic, contemporary music. His recent work includes KPop Demon Hunters’ “Golden” (Golden Glowin’ Version), which premiered on Jimmy Kimmel Live, and Jennifer Hudson’s 2025 Grammy-nominated album The Gift of Love.

He co-produced and mixed OneRepublic’s “I Ain’t Worried” from Top Gun: Maverick — now 4× Platinum with over 2 billion Spotify streams — and has contributed to multiple projects with OneRepublic, David Guetta, Shawn Mendes, Kygo, and other major artists. With 8+ billion career streams, 8 SOCAN Awards, and recognition among Jaxsta’s Top Producers globally, John brings a proven ability to create polished, cinematic productions that translate across film, trailers, advertising, and contemporary music.

Shortlisted Artist — 02 / 03

Bettina Bergström

Bettina Bergström is a Finnish composer and producer specializing in cinematic, orchestral, and trailer music. She has created remixes for Universal Music artists including Billie Eilish, Ariana Grande, The Weeknd, Norah Jones, and Maggie Rogers, with her Maggie Rogers remix featured in Google’s Year in Search campaign and nominated for an AICP Award.

Her music has been featured in campaigns and trailers for Apple TV+’s The New Look, Lionsgate’s White Bird: A Wonder Story, Hulu’s The Handmaid’s Tale, and the Magic Johnson documentary, as well as on Grey’s Anatomy. Bergström also composed the score for the feature film The Shuroo Process and has built an international career working across film, television, advertising, and recorded music.

Shortlisted Artist — 03 / 03

Isabelle Engman

Isabelle Engman is a Swedish-born, Los Angeles-based composer, producer, and pianist working across film, television, video games, and advertising. Her music blends orchestral composition, electronic textures, Nordic influences, and experimental sound design, with work recorded at Warner Brothers, Capitol Records, and EastWest Studios.

Her screen credits include Amazon/Blumhouse’s feature Madres, The Price of Peace for SVT/ARTE, Stockholm Bloodbath, The Gold Panner from Lannavaara, and the SXSW-premiering series Sterling, alongside projects for Sony Pictures and Microsoft.
