Producer / Composer

Dan Mufson

Dan has scored hundreds of campaigns for the world's most recognizable brands, with a parallel body of work across film, television, and his own artist projects.

His music has been featured in films and television series including Bad Moms, Gossip Girl (HBO Max), Atypical (Netflix), Home Before Dark (Apple TV+), Nashville, and Quantico.

As one half of the indie-pop duo Scavenger Hunt, alongside creative partner Jill Lamoureux, Dan has amassed more than 50 million streams worldwide, earning recognition from Billboard, Neon Gold, Indie Shuffle, and Pigeons & Planes.

He has collaborated with artists including Loud Luxury, RAC, The Midnight, MUNA, Betty Who, Satin Jackets, Rachel Yamagata, Regina Spektor, Capital Cities, Great Good Fine OK, Clubhouse, and Esthero.

Beyond composition and production, Dan has partnered with global brand teams to develop sonic branding systems and UX sound libraries for companies including CoreLogic and Thermo Fisher Scientific.

Producer / Composer

Sam Barbera

BEGINNERS is the alt-pop, genre-blending creative force of Sam Barbera, a multi-instrumentalist Los Angeles musician who is also one fourth of the rising band L.A. Exes.

She has made a name for herself in LA's music scene as a talented songwriter and captivating frontwoman, leading to collaborations with artists like Kygo and tours with Tove Lo and Walk The Moon.

Her ear for a great hook and left-of-center productions has led to over 55 million streams and global campaigns for many of the world's biggest brands.

BEGINNERS has also soundtracked numerous high-profile film and TV placements, including Disney's Raya and the Last Dragon (Trailer), Money Heist, Elite, The Walking Dead (Trailer), The Flight Attendant, The Sex Lives of College Girls, The L Word: Gen Q, Riverdale, Shameless, Special (Trailer), Sex/Life, Disobedience, The Happytime Murders, Queer as Folk, Lucifer, Flack, and New Girl.

To make it all the more impressive, everything the self-driven Barbera has accomplished to date has been done on her own terms as an independent artist.

Producer / Composer

Alana Da Fonseca

The daughter of Brazilian jazz drummer Duduka Da Fonseca, Alana grew up in Manhattan surrounded by a community of musicians.

After graduating cum laude from Purchase College with a degree in Music Production, she began mixing and producing at an award-winning music house in New York City, transitioning into the film and TV space in 2005.

Alana has written over 1,000 songs for TV and film. Her recent projects include music producer and arranger for the hit Netflix series Wednesday, for which she was nominated for a Grammy; series songwriter for the hit Disney series Primos; songwriter and producer for Walt Disney World's 50th Anniversary anthem, The Magic is Calling; and vocal producer for Julian Casablancas' newly released album with his group, The Voidz.

As a vocal producer she was responsible for the Julie and the Phantoms band songs, and as a music producer she has contributed to The Addams Family and Eurovision, among many others.

As a vocal coach and producer, she has worked with actors such as Bette Midler, Will Ferrell, Rebel Wilson, Liam Hemsworth, Anna Faris, and Chris Evans, and top-40 recording artists like Daft Punk, Sean Paul, Kesha, and Miley Cyrus.

Max Levin

Max Levin is a multi-platinum-selling music producer, songwriter, and pianist based in Los Angeles.

Recognized for his ear-catching melodies, exceptional sound design, and infectious drum grooves, he has worked with artists including Travis Scott, Ciara, Tinashe, Burna Boy, Wale, Macklemore, Big Freedia, and ZEROBASEONE, and landed a #1 on the Billboard 200 chart with ATEEZ last year.

With roots as a touring musician and music director, Max has honed his craft across rhythmic, pop, and world genres. He was recently tapped for New Balance's 2026 Global Campaign, creating music arrangements across the various hero spots.

Producer / Composer

Caleb Schultz

Caleb Schultz is a Platinum songwriter, music producer, and composer whose work spans film, television, advertising, and artist projects.

Originally from Little Rock, Arkansas, Schultz spent a decade in Los Angeles developing his career before relocating to the Nashville area.

His music has been featured in major trailers and promotional campaigns for platforms including Netflix, Disney, ABC, and Peacock, as well as in commercials and television series across Hulu, Fox, and Amazon Prime. His work has also appeared in a Super Bowl commercial, cementing his reputation for delivering high-impact, emotionally driven music.

In addition to his commercial success, Schultz has composed original scores for award-winning short films such as Myra and Waterdawg. He continues to work across documentary series, film, and high-level media productions, where he often serves as both composer and music supervisor.

Known for his versatility and instinct for storytelling, Schultz blends cinematic scale with modern production, consistently delivering music that connects with audiences across genres and platforms.

Producer / Composer

Allister X

Michael MacAllister is a Los Angeles-based composer, songwriter, and producer whose journey began in New York City.

An internship at Human Music and Sound Design launched his career as a staff composer and later as creative director at Butter Music and Sound. He scored numerous ad campaigns before going freelance in 2015, writing and producing Donna Missal's Spotify viral hit "Keep Lying."

Since then, he has collaborated with artists such as Labrinth, Sia, Lizzo, and Alicia Keys, and is currently working on UK artist Rationale's upcoming album.

Producing under the moniker ALLISTER X, Mike's recent work was featured in a Novartis commercial that premiered during the Super Bowl of 2025. The spot featured Wanda Sykes and garnered four Clio Awards. His work has also appeared in the film The Fall Guy, the trailer for The Beekeeper, and campaigns for Apple, Google, Pepsi, Lexus, and Walmart.

His film credits include Freakonomics: The Movie, Impossible Monsters, and Reeling. He composed themes for ESPN's Mike and Mike and Outside the Lines, and crafted sonic identities for Encore, Paramount Network, and Wells Fargo.

Mike's trailer work includes scoring for high-profile projects like Dune: Part Two, Bodies Bodies Bodies, King Richard, and HBO's Euphoria Season 2 and Winning Time. His ability to blend emotional depth with commercial appeal has made him a sought-after composer across industries.

Martin Wave

Martin Wave is a Swedish-born producer, composer, and artist based in Los Angeles.

Over his 14-year career, he has worked with stars like Post Malone, Vince Staples, Kacey Musgraves, and Paloma Faith. His accolades include contributions to NCT DREAM's global #1 album "Hot Sauce" and a Best Score win at Filmquest (2023).

His music has powered iconic campaigns for Apple, Marvel, Porsche, and Gatorade, as well as trailers for Killers of the Flower Moon, Fast X, Civilization VII, and Fortnite.

Martin also co-founded PARASOL with his wife, Grace Hong. This creative community and label empowers purpose-driven artists to push boundaries and craft lasting, impactful work.
